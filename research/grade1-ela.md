# Grade 1 ELA research: Amplify CKLA at AACPS, plus Maryland grade 1 ELA standards

Pass dated 2026-10-22. It covers the 2026-27 school year, for a first grader in Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS), Maryland.

## Method and limits

- **No primary document was opened.** WebFetch returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for amplify.com, rebuild.prod.amplify.com, amplify-com-mktg.imgix.net, coreknowledge.org, about.readworks.org and fliphtml5.com. curl got a 403 CONNECT rejection from the egress proxy for amplify.com, coreknowledge.org, edreports.org and engageny.org. The task brief hoped non-Maryland hosts would work. They did not.
- All evidence comes from WebSearch result summaries that quote or paraphrase the documents. About 36 searches were used, over the budget of about 25. The extra searches went to the edition question, because it changes the answer.
- Verdict scale (the same as the earlier report):
  - **confirmed**: two or more independent sources agree, at least one of them Amplify, Core Knowledge, EdReports or the state, and nothing contradicts them.
  - **partly**: the core holds but details are thin or conflict.
  - **unverified**: only one weak source, or none.
  - **refuted**: the evidence contradicts the claim.
- Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT) listings, blogs and AI-written meeting digests (citizenportal.ai) are treated as secondary sources. On their own they never make a claim confirmed.

## Headline: there are two CKLA editions, and the Grade 1 sequences differ

The task brief's expected list ("Fables and Stories, The Human Body, Different Lands Similar Stories, Early World Civilizations, ... Frontier Explorers") is the **2nd Edition / Core Knowledge** Grade 1 sequence, and it checks out. But **Amplify CKLA 3rd Edition** came out for **2025-26** with a **restructured Grade 1 Knowledge sequence**:
- a new Geography unit;
- two district-choice units;
- a research unit;
- 165 instructional days for K-2 Knowledge.

Frontier Explorers does not appear in what was found for it.

**Which edition AACPS first-grade classrooms use in 2026-27 was not found.** AACPS school newsletters from 2024-25 match 2nd Edition sequences in grades 2 and 5. Nothing from 2025-26 or 2026-27 settles it.

**This also matters for the K, grade 3 and grade 5 sequences already in CURRICULUM-NOTES.md.** Those are 2nd Edition sequences. Kindergarten in particular may differ under 3E: ReadWorks shows 3E kindergarten titles such as "Star Light, Star Bright: Nursery Rhymes and Fables", and Amplify says Geography is new in K-1.

**Practical recommendation.** Build Grade 1 packs on the union of the two editions. Nearly every topic appears in both. The exceptions are Geography (3E only) and Frontier Explorers (2E only). Do not number the packs by domain.

### Claim A2. What each 2E domain covers (content for writing questions)

**Verdict: confirmed for D1, D2, D4, D8, D9, D10 and D11. Partly confirmed for D3, D5 and D6. Unverified for D7's contents.**

- **D1 Fables and Stories.** EdReports: "ten literary stories, six of them well-known fables and four classic folktales". A 3E guide (Hudson) names "The Boy Who Cried Wolf", "The Goose and the Golden Eggs" and "The Fox and the Grapes" among its fables.
- **D2 The Human Body.** EdReports: ten informational texts, with "a pediatrician character reinforcing basic ideas about the body". The 3E version (Hudson) covers the body's systems (skeletal, muscular, digestive, circulatory, nervous), the major organs, and germs and disease.
- **D3 Different Lands, Similar Stories.** Core Knowledge: "Students encounter different cultures from around the world as they explore the ways in which folktales from different lands treat similar themes or characters". Search summaries mention Cinderella variants, tiny heroes and tricksters. The specific tales were not retrieved.
- **D4 Early World Civilizations.** Core Knowledge, quoted:
  - Mesopotamia: "the importance of the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, the development of cuneiform as the earliest-known form of writing, the first codification of laws known as the Code of Hammurabi, and the significance of gods and goddesses in the 'cradle of civilization'".
  - Egypt: "the importance of the Nile River; the use of hieroglyphs; the rise of pharaohs, including Tutankhamun and Hatshepsut; the building of the Sphinx and pyramids; and the significance of mummification and the afterlife".
  - *Not retrieved:* whether the 2E domain also has its world-religions section.
- **D5 Early American Civilizations.** A district CKLA page: "the fundamental features of the Maya, Aztec, and Inca, including farming, the establishment of cities and government, as well as religion."
- **D6 Astronomy.** Core Knowledge: Earth "in relation to the moon, the other planets, the sun, and the stars". The 3E version (Hudson) adds major constellations.
- **D7 The History of the Earth.** The title is confirmed. Its contents (likely rocks, fossils and dinosaurs) were **not retrieved**.
- **D8 Animals and Habitats.** EdReports: eight informational texts "such as 'Animals of the Arctic Habitat' and 'Animals of the Saltwater Habitat'". Core Knowledge: "the interconnectedness of living things with their physical environment".
- **D9 Fairy Tales.** EdReports: nine literary texts "including 'Sleeping Beauty' and 'Rapunzel'". Students "learn about the Brothers Grimm, identify common elements of fairy tales, consider problems and solutions ... compare and contrast different tales."
- **D10 A New Nation: American Independence.** EdReports: "one literary text and eleven informational texts such as 'The New World' and 'A Young Nation is Born.'"
- **D11 Frontier Explorers.** EdReports: "eleven nonfiction texts such as 'Crossing the Appalachian Mountains' and 'Red Cedars and Grizzly Bears'", covering "Daniel Boone, Lewis and Clark, and Sacagawea". A Core Knowledge guide adds Louisiana Territory objectives.

### Claim D2. The phonics content of each Skills unit

**Verdict: partly confirmed.** The unit-level focus is confirmed from Core Knowledge and Amplify pages. Individual sound-spellings in Units 3-7 come partly from lesson lists in secondary sources.

- **Unit 1 (review of kindergarten).** Amplify scope and sequence: "the Basic Code consonant sounds and spellings and the five short vowel sounds and spellings taught in Kindergarten CKLA Units 3-9 are reviewed in Grade 1 CKLA Unit 1."
  - Curriculum-map outcome: "Identify 5 short vowel sounds and 25 consonant/consonant digraph sounds".
  - Digraphs ch, sh, th and ng appear around lessons 17-20 ("Lesson 17 Sounds /ch/, /sh/ Spelled 'ch', 'sh'").
  - The unit introduces **Tricky Spellings** (spellings with more than one pronunciation) and **Tricky Words**, for example "Lesson 19 Tricky Words: the, who".
  - App content: CVC words with short vowels, consonant digraphs, tricky words.
- **Units 2-4 teach the rest of the Basic Code vowels.** Amplify scope and sequence: "the remaining Basic Code vowel sounds and spellings are taught in Grade 1 Units 2—4."
  - **Unit 2 (Gran):** "introduces five vowel sounds and the most common (or least ambiguous) spelling for each sound". Students "read and write words with separated digraphs (such as a and e in cake)". These are the long vowels spelled a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e and u_e. *Inferred:* the five separated digraphs are the standard set, but only "a_e as in cake" was quoted. **[Corrected in Verification: the /ee/ spelling taught in Unit 2 is 'ee', not 'e_e'. The set is 'ee', 'a_e', 'i_e', 'o_e', 'u_e'.]**
  - **Unit 3 (Fables):** "five vowel sounds and the most common spelling for each sound, five new Tricky Words, and the Tricky Spelling 'oo'". A lesson list shows 'ou' > /ou/, 'oi' > /oi/ and 'aw' > /aw/, with review of the two sounds of 'oo' (as in soon and look). Writing begins with the narrative writing process. Grammar covers verbs and present, past and future tense. *Unconfirmed:* /ee/ 'ee' looks like earlier code rather than a Unit 3 target.
  - **Unit 4 (The Green Fern Zoo):** "the most common (or least ambiguous) spellings for /r/-controlled vowel sounds". A lesson list shows 'er' > /er/, 'ar' > /ar/ and 'or' > /or/.
- **Units 5-7 teach spelling alternatives (the Advanced Code).**
  - **Unit 5 (Kate's Book):** "spelling alternatives that make up the advanced code". Scope-and-sequence lines include "'ai' and 'ay' > /ae/ (with Cues)" (Lesson 1) and "'oa' > /oe/" (Lesson 3). These are vowel teams. *Not confirmed:* 'ow' for /oe/ and 'ea' for /ee/. **[Corrected in Verification: Unit 5 teaches consonant spelling alternatives (/ch/ 'tch', /j/ 'g' 'ge', /v/ 've', /r/ 'wr', plus a review of /k/ 'c' 'k' 'ck' 'cc'). The 'ai'/'ay' and 'oa' lessons belong to Unit 7.]**
  - **Unit 6 (Grace):** "students continue work with several spelling alternatives for consonant sounds". Examples seen in lesson lists: 'p'/'pp' for /p/, and 'c'/'k'/'cc'/'ck' for /k/. These may belong to Unit 5 or Unit 6; the snippet was ambiguous. **[Resolved in Verification: the /k/ review ('c', 'k', 'ck', 'cc') is Unit 5, Lessons 4-5. Unit 6 includes the Tricky Spelling 'c' for /k/ and /s/ (Lesson 2).]**
  - **Unit 7 (Kay and Martez):** "spelling alternatives for vowel sounds", plus conjunctions, commas and noun-verb agreement. The texts are 100% decodable and written in chapter-book style.

The limits were the same as the researcher's: WebFetch and curl could not reach about.readworks.org, learning.amplify.com or aacps.org (ENOTFOUND, or a 403 from the proxy). ### Errors corrected (also flagged inline above)

1. **Skills Unit 2 (Gran): the /ee/ spelling is 'ee', not 'e_e'.** The Core Knowledge Unit 2 overview says Unit 2 introduces five vowel sounds, "including /ee/ spelled 'ee' and /ae/ spelled 'a_e'". A G1 scope and sequence lists Lesson 1 as the vowel digraph 'ee' and Lesson 3 as 'a_e'. The set is 'ee', 'a_e', 'i_e', 'o_e', 'u_e'. The unit has 19 lessons.
2. **Skills Unit 5 (Kate's Book) teaches consonant spelling alternatives, not vowel teams.** Sources:
   - Amplify's Grade 1 Curriculum Map gives the Unit 5 outcomes as alternate spellings for /ch/, /j/, /v/ and /r/, plus plural nouns and suffixes.
   - A teacher-material copy pairs /ch/ with ch or tch, /j/ with j, g or ge, /v/ with v or ve, and /r/ with r, rr or wr.
   - The TEA Unit 5 guide shows /ch/ 'tch' in Lesson 6, and Lessons 4-5 review /k/ spelled 'c', 'k', 'ck' and 'cc'.
   - The Unit 7 overview says: "you will now turn from spelling alternatives for consonant sounds to spelling alternatives for vowel sounds."
   - The "Introduce /ae/ > 'ai' and 'ay'" lesson is **Unit 7, Lesson 1** in the Unit 7 teacher guide. The researcher had it as Unit 5, Lesson 1.
3. **The RF.1.3 to CKLA mapping, corrected:**
   - final -e: Unit 2
   - the vowel team 'ee': Unit 2
   - the other long-vowel teams (ai/ay, oa and so on): Unit 7
   - the other vowel digraphs (oo, ou, oi, aw): Unit 3
   - syllables and two-syllable words, which match RF.1.3d-e, plus -ed endings: Unit 4
   - plurals and suffixes, which match RF.1.3f: Unit 5
4. **The phonics pack list in the content map is corrected** (Packs 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7). Packs 5 and 6 are consonant alternatives. Pack 7 holds the vowel teams.
5. **The "Seth" conflict is resolved.** *Seth* is the reader for **Kindergarten** Skills Unit 7 (Core Knowledge: "CKLA Unit 7: Kindergarten Skills—Seth", 17 lessons). It is not a Grade 1 reader. The school page was wrong, and Kate's Book stands.
6. **B3 ("Adventure on the High Seas: Treasure Island") is refuted as a Grade 1 unit.** *Treasure Island* is the CKLA **Grade 4** Unit 8 (Core Knowledge, the Amplify G4_U08 teacher guide, an APS G4 Unit 8 letter). The snippet the researcher saw almost certainly belonged to the Grade 4 strand.

  - The read-alouds are narrated by the pediatrician Dr. Welbody.
  - They cover five systems: skeletal, muscular, digestive, circulatory and nervous.
  - They cover germs and vaccines, with Edward Jenner and Louis Pasteur.
  - The "five keys to health" are eating right, keeping clean, exercising, sleeping enough and regular checkups.
